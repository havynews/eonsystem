What is solar technology?

Solar energy is the conversion of sunlight into energy. The sun's energy can be used to generate electricity or heat. Photovoltaics (PV) is used to generate electricity, and utility-scale PV systems are typically located on solar farms. Using a "local" energy source, such as the sun, takes advantage of our abundant natural resources to generate clean energy and reduces our dependence on harmful fossil fuels.

What We Do

Our in-house expertise spans development, construction, and operation of solar farms. We work with leading technology partners to deliver high-performance energy solutions.

Why E.ON?

Our vision is to create a world where everyone has access to affordable, zero-carbon energy. By working together with stakeholders at all levels, we are developing utility-scale solar farms that bring us closer to a clean energy future.
